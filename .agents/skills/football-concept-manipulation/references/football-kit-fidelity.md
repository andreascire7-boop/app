## Why this file exists

This is the one place in the whole concept where invention is not allowed. Everything else in the artwork (environment, scale, atmosphere, props, metaphor) is open to full creative freedom — the player and the kit are not. Re-read this file before finalizing any concept involving a real player.

## Player identity — preserve

- Face and facial structure
- Skin tone and texture
- Hair and hairstyle
- Body proportions and physique
- Recognizable physical characteristics
- Anatomy: limbs, hands, feet in correct proportion and pose

## Player identity — never

- Never redesign the player's appearance
- Never make the player more muscular or otherwise alter physique
- Never change body proportions
- Never change facial characteristics
- Never substitute a different person while implying it's the named player

Creative manipulation happens *around* the player (environment, styling context, scale, lighting) — not *to* the player's identity or anatomy.

## Kit — preserve exactly, whenever visible

- Club crest
- Sponsor (exact wording and position)
- Manufacturer logo
- Stripes/pattern geometry and colors
- Collar and sleeve details
- Competition/league patches
- Player number
- Player name/lettering
- Shorts and socks
- Boots, where relevant to the shot

## Kit — never

- Never invent a sponsor or sponsor wording
- Never replace or redesign the club crest
- Never redesign the jersey pattern
- Never change the player's number or name
- Never modify stripe/pattern geometry
- Never invent a detail you can't verify from a reference
- Never replace visible kit typography with placeholder or invented text

## When a detail is uncertain

If a specific detail (exact sponsor wording, patch design, current kit variant) can't be reliably confirmed from research, don't guess or invent it — use the clearest available reference image for that detail as-is (per the sourcing rules in [visual-research.md](visual-research.md)) rather than fabricating a plausible-looking substitute.
