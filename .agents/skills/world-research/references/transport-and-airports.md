## Transfer time components

For any airport-to-destination question, research and state separately:
- Distance (see [geographic-analysis.md](geographic-analysis.md) for distance-type distinctions)
- Realistic transfer time by each relevant mode (public transit, taxi/ride-hailing, rental car), including typical wait/transfer time, not just in-vehicle time
- Cost per mode
- Which mode(s) actually operate at the traveler's arrival/departure time (see checklist below)

## Late-night/early-morning checklist

**If arrival or departure is outside roughly 06:00-23:00 local time, explicitly verify — never assume:**

- [ ] Does public transport (train/metro/bus) actually operate at that hour? Check the transit agency's own schedule, not a general assumption that "cities have transit".
- [ ] Is there a night bus or dedicated airport night service, and does it run on the specific day of the week (weekend schedules often differ)?
- [ ] Is licensed taxi/ride-hailing available 24/7 at that airport, and what's the realistic wait time at that hour?
- [ ] Is the airport terminal itself open, or does it close between flights (relevant for very early departures or overnight layovers)?
- [ ] If nothing operates, what are the traveler's actual options (wait at airport, pre-booked transfer, airport hotel)?

State the answer explicitly in the output rather than silently assuming transport will be available — this is one of the most common practical-planning failure points.

## Sources

- Airport authority's own website (transfer options page)
- Local transit agency (schedules, especially night/weekend schedules)
- Ride-hailing app availability in that specific city/airport (some cities restrict airport pickup zones or don't have ride-hailing at all)
