## Trip cost formula

```
total = transport + accommodation + local_transport + food + activities + fees
```

Rules:
- State whether every number is **per person** or **total for the group** — never mix without labeling.
- Include baggage fees, resort/city/tourist taxes, booking fees, and mandatory insurance when they apply — these are often omitted from headline prices.
- Multiply nightly accommodation by actual nights, not by trip length (arrival/departure days may differ).
- For multi-city trips, break the formula out per leg, then sum.

## Flight research

- Search on the actual travel dates, not "cheapest month" placeholders, unless the user has date flexibility.
- Check flexible-date views (±3 days) only if the user indicated flexibility; otherwise price the exact dates asked.
- Verify the final price on the airline's own site or a reputable aggregator that shows all fees — headline search prices frequently exclude baggage, seat selection, or payment fees.
- For connections, check minimum connection time at the transfer airport and note the risk if it's tight, especially across different airlines/terminals.
- State the currency and the date the price was checked; flight prices change constantly and are `VERIFIED` only as of that check, not as a guarantee.

## Accommodation total-cost check

- Compare **total cost for the stay**, not just nightly rate — cleaning fees, service fees, and city/tourist taxes can add 15-30%+ to short stays.
- Note cancellation policy and payment timing (pay now vs. pay at property) since they affect real cost/risk.
- State whether a rate is per room or per person, and how many people it covers.
