# Landing pages & marketing sites

## Contents
- Structure

## Structure

Build in this order, including only sections that earn their place for this specific product:

1. Clear value proposition (what it does, for whom, why it matters — in the first screen)
2. Strong hero (headline + supporting line + primary CTA, not a wall of text)
3. Proof (logos, metrics, testimonials — whatever is genuinely available)
4. Product explanation (how it works, concretely)
5. Benefits (outcomes for the user, not just feature lists)
6. Differentiation (why this vs. alternatives)
7. Social proof, if available (case studies, reviews, usage numbers)
8. Primary CTA repeated at a natural decision point
9. Objections/FAQ, where real objections exist
10. Final CTA

Skip sections that don't have real content behind them — a "Why choose us" section with three generic icon+paragraph blocks and no substance is a generic-design red flag (see the audit in [ux-review.md](ux-review.md)).

## Copy and CTAs

- Be specific about the value delivered — avoid generic CTA language ("Get Started", "Unlock Potential", "Learn More") when a more specific action is possible ("Start free trial", "See pricing", "Book a demo").
- Match headline/subhead length to how much attention that section will realistically get — hero copy should be scannable in seconds.
- Vary section layout/rhythm rather than repeating the same icon-heading-paragraph pattern for every feature block — repetition without variation is the clearest generic-template signal.
